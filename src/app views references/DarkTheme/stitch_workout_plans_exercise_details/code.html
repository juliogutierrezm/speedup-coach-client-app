Detailed Description

The barbell bench press is the gold standard for developing upper body power and muscle mass. By lying on a flat bench and pressing a barbell away from your body, you engage the pectoralis major, anterior deltoids, and triceps brachii in a synchronized movement.

1

Setup

Lie flat, grip the bar slightly wider than shoulders. Eyes should be directly under the bar.

2

The Descent

Lower the bar under control to your mid-sternum while inhaling and keeping elbows at 45 degrees.

3

The Press

Push the bar back to the starting position with explosive force, exhaling as you clear the sticking point.
