Execution

The barbell bench press is the gold standard for developing upper body power. It engages the pectoralis major, anterior deltoids, and triceps.

1

Setup

Lie flat, grip the bar slightly wider than shoulders. Eyes directly under the bar.

2

The Descent

Lower under control to mid-sternum. Keep elbows at 45 degrees.

3

The Press

Push back to start with explosive force. Exhale on the way up.
